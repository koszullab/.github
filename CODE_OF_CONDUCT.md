# Code of conduct

The repositories of koszullab follow the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
in their issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere else they are represented.

In short: be welcoming and considerate, criticise work rather than people, and accept that a
decision may not go your way. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing anyone's private
information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code privately to the maintainer, Jacques Serizay, at
<jacques.serizay@pasteur.fr>. Every report is read, answered and kept confidential. The maintainers may edit or
remove contributions, and restrict or ban participants, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines
describe.
